# AI 起标题

## 背景

热载流子短波红外探测器的文章，目标期刊：*Science*、*Nature*、*Nature Photonics*、*Nature Communications*、*Science Advances*。

> [!question] 核心矛盾
> 亮点是完全 **Foundry-compatible** 的工艺流程（与其他探测器相比，产线兼容性远优于其他材料），但希望更多强调**科学性**——实际性能还不能和商用红外探测器比。

### 原始标题

> Sensitive hot carrier PD employing foundry compatible materials and processes

**评价**：太弱，吹得不够狠，不能引起广泛兴趣。

---

## 原始提示词

```prompt
我现在有一篇热载流子短波红外探测器的文章  我希望的投稿期刊是Science Nature Nature Photonics Nature Communicates Science Advances这类期刊；我原本拟定的标题是Sensitive hot carrier PD employing foundry compatible materials and processes 现在我感觉这个标题太弱了 吹的不够狠 不能引起广泛的兴趣。我认为我文章的亮点是完全Foundry compatibile 的工艺流程（与其他探测器对比 与产线兼容性远远好于其他材料）但同时我希望更多的强调其科学性，因为在实际上的性能还是不能和商用的红外探测器比。请依照我提供的信息为我的文章起几个有吸引力的标题供我参考
```

## AI 回复：标题备选

### A. 克制但更有分量（适合 Nat. Comms / Sci. Adv.）

1. **Foundry-compatible hot-carrier photodetectors for short-wave infrared sensing**
2. **CMOS-foundry-process hot-carrier photodetection in the short-wave infrared**
3. **Scalable hot-carrier short-wave infrared photodetectors enabled by foundry-compatible materials and processes**
4. **Wafer-scale, foundry-compatible hot-carrier photodetectors for short-wave infrared**
5. **A foundry-compatible platform for hot-carrier short-wave infrared photodetection**

### B. 平衡：强调"科学机制 + 可制造性"（更像 Nat. Photon.）

6. **Hot-carrier-enabled short-wave infrared photodetection in a fully foundry-compatible process flow**
7. **Foundry-ready hot-carrier photodetection: a scalable route to short-wave infrared sensing**
8. **Engineering hot-carrier extraction for short-wave infrared photodetection with foundry-compatible processing**
9. **A CMOS-compatible hot-carrier photodetection mechanism for short-wave infrared at scale**
10. **Foundry-compatible hot-carrier extraction unlocks short-wave infrared photodetection on-chip**

### C. 更"狠"：制造冲击力（偏 Science / Nature 风格）

11. **Bringing hot-carrier short-wave infrared photodetection to the foundry**
12. **Foundry-scale hot-carrier photodetectors for short-wave infrared**
13. **A foundry-compatible route to hot-carrier short-wave infrared photodetection**
14. **Hot-carrier photodetection meets CMOS manufacturing in the short-wave infrared**
15. **From lab physics to foundry lines: hot-carrier short-wave infrared photodetectors**

### D. 突出"产业化/平台化"叙事

16. **A foundry-manufacturable hot-carrier photodetector platform for short-wave infrared imaging and sensing**
17. **Foundry-compatible materials and processes for scalable hot-carrier short-wave infrared photodetectors**
18. **Monolithic, foundry-compatible hot-carrier short-wave infrared photodetectors for integrated sensing**

### E. 突出"科学问题/新范式"

19. **Non-equilibrium hot-carrier extraction as a foundry-compatible strategy for short-wave infrared photodetection**
20. **Hot-carrier photodetection in the short-wave infrared enabled by manufacturable device physics**

## 顶刊标题小建议

- **避免缩写**：把 PD 改成 *photodetector*（除非领域共识非常强）
- **升级定位词**：尽量用 `foundry-compatible` / `CMOS-compatible` / `wafer-scale` / `monolithic` / `scalable` / `platform`，把亮点从"器件一例"升级为"可复制的路线/平台"
- **加入机制动词**：`enables` / `unlocks` / `engineering` / `extraction`
- 如需进一步精准定制，可补充：器件核心结构关键词、应用场景、最能站得住脚的指标
